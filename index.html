Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Guess and Num Quiz.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Information We Handle

Game progress: Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not send this information to our own servers unless we clearly explain otherwise when introducing a feature.

Problem reports: If you submit a problem report, the information you choose to provide and the information required by the form may be sent to us, such as the relevant image or level name and your description of the problem.

Advertising: The game may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads, if included in the version you use. These providers may process advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interaction or usage data to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, or comply with legal requirements. Their processing is governed by their own policies and available privacy settings.

2. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on third-party services. Those providers handle information under their own privacy policies. Please review the policies of any advertising or other third-party service included in the version of the game you use.

3. Data Storage and Deletion

Game progress and settings stored locally remain on your device until the game data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data may permanently remove that local progress. Information submitted through a third-party service is handled according to that provider's policies and tools.

4. Children's Privacy

Guess and Num Quiz is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Depending on the player's age, location, and applicable law, advertising and data processing may be limited or handled differently. If you believe personal information has been submitted to us inappropriately, contact us using the address below.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to handle information responsibly. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party providers are responsible for the security practices of their own services.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its features, or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
